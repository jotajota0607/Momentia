About Us

Who We Are

Momentia LLC is a professionally managed retail and distribution company headquartered in Texas. We specialize in sourcing, purchasing, inventory management, and retail distribution across multiple consumer product categories.

Our mission is to build lasting relationships with trusted suppliers and brands while delivering operational excellence throughout the purchasing and fulfillment process.

We are committed to conducting business with integrity, transparency, and respect for the policies and standards established by our partners.

Our Mission

To create lasting value for suppliers, brands, and customers through reliable purchasing practices, responsible inventory management, and professional retail operations.
